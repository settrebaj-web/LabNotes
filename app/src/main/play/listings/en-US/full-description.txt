LabNotes is a simple and fast notes app.

• Add a note with one tap
• Instant search across all notes
• Delete notes you no longer need
• No sign-up: notes are stored only on your device
• Light and dark theme

We never collect your notes or share them with third parties. To improve stability the app sends anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics (Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics).

Student project for the "Mobile App Development Technologies" course, AUES.
